# JournalXI data notes

JournalXI keeps its mission and quest guides under `data/` so they remain
available without a network connection. CatsEyeXI's existing quest-log and
mission IDs are retained wherever possible so Active and Completed detection
continues to match the server.

## Quest data

Quest metadata and walkthroughs for San d'Oria, Bastok, Windurst, Jeuno, Other,
Outlands, Aht Urhgan, Crystal War, Adoulin, and Abyssea were generated from
their corresponding BG-Wiki pages with `tools/scrape_sandoria_quests.py` and
`tools/build_sandoria_quests.py`.

- Other combines the Other, Selbina, Mhaura, Tavnazian Safehold, and Mog House
  categories.
- Aht Urhgan combines the parent category with Al Zahbi, Whitegate, and Nashmau.
- `Unity Concord` is retained as a CatsEyeXI-specific reference entry.
- Guides without game quest IDs remain readable but do not have automatic
  status detection.
- Entries filed under a different in-game quest log retain that game's category
  even when BG-Wiki also lists them elsewhere.
- Abyssea contains all 192 BG-Wiki quest pages mapped to quest IDs 0 through
  191.

BG-Wiki currently has no complete walkthrough for a small number of quests.
JournalXI retains its previous guide or uses a matching guide from another
credited FFXI wiki for those entries.

## Mission data

The nation, expansion, scenario, Abyssea, Rhapsodies, and Voracious Resurgence
walkthroughs are generated by the mission builders under `tools/`.

Several entries intentionally preserve custom or existing guides where a wiki
page is absent, empty, split across multiple branches, or incompatible with the
server-facing tracker state. These include:

- The three nation rank 2-3 branching missions.
- San d'Oria 3-3's complete first-time Delkfutt's Tower route.
- Bastok 8-1.
- Promathia's combined `Three Paths` guide.
- WotG's `A Dreamy Interlude`.
- Adoulin's `An Emergency Convocation` and mission 5-2-2.
- Kupo Mission 12.
- Any mission whose BG-Wiki page currently has no walkthrough content.

The original pre-rebuild mission data is preserved by the annotated Git tag
`missions-original-before-bgwiki`.

## Testing status

Assault and Campaign Ops data loads correctly but has not been verified through
live completion of every operation. Campaign Ops requires particular attention
to nation-specific NPCs, locations, enemies, and entries for which BG-Wiki has
no dedicated national page.

Other gameplay information was originally assembled from sources credited by
Journal, including BG-Wiki and related FFXI addon projects. Source-file comments
retain additional attribution where applicable.
